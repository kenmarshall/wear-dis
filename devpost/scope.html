Outfit Advisor

Tell it about an event. It recommends ranked, complete outfits, explains why each one works, and links every piece to Amazon. (working name)

The unique kernel

It gives you the confidence to pick by explaining why each outfit fits, ranked best first.

Pinterest shows good-looking outfits but assumes you already know which one fits and why. This advisor makes the style call for you.

The core loop

Who it's for

You, first. Someone who doesn't know styles or which colors go together and wants an advisor to decide, not a gallery to sort through.

"Sometimes I can't decide what to wear."

Feel

Like Pinterest outfit boards: visual, put-together, browsable. The tone of a knowledgeable friend who reassures you, not a catalog.

The boundary

NOW In the proof of concept
  • Free-text event description, followed by a few clarifying questions
  • About five complete outfits (top, bottom, footwear, accessories), ranked, each with "why it works"
  • Light visuals: outfit cards that list the pieces with color swatches, not photos
  • Light links: an Amazon search link per piece, built from its description and color
  • New items only
LATER Worth doing, just not now
  • Generated images of a person wearing each outfit, perhaps only for the selected one
  • Seeing yourself in the outfits
  • Using clothes you already own, mixed with new items or with other owned pieces
  • Style options as a choice, in addition to the recommended style
CUT Named, with reasons
  • Exact products through Amazon's API: it needs an approved affiliate account with sales history. Search links cover the need.
  • AI outfit images in the PoC: slow, cost per image, and wouldn't match the linked products. Swatch cards prove the kernel.
